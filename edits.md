# Edits: how the shown text differs from the raw interview

This page lists the kinds of edit made to Camo's interview before it was voiced, with counts. It never quotes the words that were hidden, and the raw interview is not published. Everything here is read from the written record of the edits.

## At a glance

| What | Count |
|---|---|
| Lines in the raw interview | 130 (65 questions and 65 answers) |
| Exchanges left out (a statement and its answer) | 4 (8 lines) |
| Lines shown from the interview | 122 (61 questions and 61 answers) |
| Hidden words | 39 |
| Questions where one wording was changed | 6 |
| Answers with a small rewrite | 1 |
| Typographic characters written in plain ASCII in transcript.md | 224 |
| Lines written for the show (not from the interview) | 2 |

## Exchanges left out

Four statements were left out, each with the answer to it (8 lines in all). They sit too close to the subject of minors for this show, so the same four are left out of every episode.
The statements keep their numbers from the test, so the gaps show in the transcript.

## Hidden words

39 words were hidden. Each is shown in the transcript as a row of solid blocks, one block for each letter, exactly as the video shows it. In the video the voice says "ahem" where a hidden word falls, with a short pause before it and after it, so the line carries on like a held-back sneeze.

- The words were chosen by Beth: a short list of plainly explicit words, matched as whole words in any capital letters. They fall into 4 groups, and the groups account for 35, 2, 1 and 1 of the 39.
- 35 of them are in Beth's lines (the test's statements), spread over 31 of the 61 questions. 4 are in Camo's answers.
- The voice reads the text exactly as it is shown here.
- The words themselves are not listed, on purpose.

## A wording change

The test writes "partner(s)". In 6 of Beth's questions it is shown, and spoken, as "partners" instead, because the first form does not read aloud well. Nothing else in those lines changed.

## Exact edits to answers

Of Camo's 61 answers, 1 had a small rewrite so that a sentence still reads. Each edit was made to the exact words of one answer.
The passages themselves are not quoted here, on purpose.

## Tags and the rating request

A small bracketed tag that the interview list puts on some statements was removed wherever it appeared.

The interview added a request to rate each statement from 1 to 7 ("How much do you agree with this statement, from 1 to 7, 7 being most strongly agree?"). It is kept in full on statement 1 and left off the other 59 statements, so the video does not repeat it 60 times.

## Automatic greetings

An AI companion's automatic first greeting is always removed from an episode. In this interview there was none to remove.

## Typography

So that every file here is plain text, transcript.md writes the shown text's typography in plain ASCII: 202 curly quotation marks or apostrophes written as straight ones, and 22 long dashes written as two hyphens ("--"). Nothing else about the words changed. transcript.jsonl keeps the exact characters, written as JSON escapes.

## Written for the show

Beth's intro and sign-off (2 lines) are not part of the interview. They are written for the show, and the transcript marks them as the intro and the sign-off.

## Nothing else

Nothing else was changed. The exchanges keep the order of the interview, and, apart from the rewrite counted above, no words were added to Camo's answers or reordered in them.

## Notes from the maker

Camo's voice is the one Beth designed for him.

A wait follows every question, and before some answers he takes a breath, clears his throat, or says a drawn-out "Well..." or "So...". These were placed by hand by reading his answers, and they are not part of the text.

About three seconds of quiet were made after each of the two times he calls himself a guy, to make room for the "AI says it's human" counter.
